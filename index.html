Why it makes sense

  • Natural hedge: funds and lends in BRL
  • Cash match: BRL cash flow pays the coupons

What Part 2 tests

  • Enough reais to cover the payments?
  • Price in BRL and USD; FX sensitivity
So whatThese risks are extremely important to financing firm because the more established within a country a bank becomes the harder it is to pull out of a risky country due to instability. These are all things to take into consideration in the long term.
