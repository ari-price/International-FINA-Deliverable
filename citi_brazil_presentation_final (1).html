08 | Why Issue Local Debt

A BRL bond offsets Citi's BRL exposure, as long as its BRL cash flows can cover the payments

Why it makes sense

  • Natural hedge: Citi Brazil funds with BRL deposits and lends in BRL, so BRL borrowing adds little new currency risk
  • Cash-flow match: excess BRL operating cash flow can pay the semiannual coupon and principal without buying reais

What Part 2 must show

  • Whether Citi receives enough reais to cover coupons and principal
  • The bond's price in BRL and USD, and its sensitivity to the real
So whatThese risks are extremely important to financing firm because the more established within a country a bank becomes the harder it is to pull out of a risky country due to instability. These are all things to take into consideration in the long term.
